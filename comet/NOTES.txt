## Spot price:

80/20 pool (80 BLNT / 20 USDC) =>

spot = 4*bal_usdc / bal_blnt

## Swap Scripts

usage:

```
./swap-usdc.sh 5 0.0070
```

Attempts to swap 5 USDC to BLNT for less than 0.0070 USDC per BLNT, and then
swaps it back on the SDEX attempting to make a profit.


```
./swap-blnd.sh 1500 152
```

Attempts to swap 1500 BLNT to USDC for less than 152 BLNT per USDC, and then
swaps it back on the SDEX attempting to make a profit.

(swap-blnd.sh keeps its old name but now swaps BLNT.)

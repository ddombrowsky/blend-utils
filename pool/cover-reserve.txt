Blent Cover Reserve Fund: covered pool
======================================

A small, separate BLNT v3 pool (XLM, USTRY, USDC) whose deposits are
covered against contract exploit and theft by an off-chain-code reserve
account: the Blent Cover Reserve Fund.  General v3 deploy steps and
reference numbers are in ../pool-deploy.txt; this file only records what
is different for this pool.

Design rules (DECISIONS 2026-11-12)
-----------------------------------

Cover rule:
    fund USDC  >=  0.90 x USD value of pool deposits
    i.e. deposit limit = fund / 0.9   (90 USDC fund -> $100 of deposits)

    "Deposits" = everything supplied to the pool (b_supply x b_rate),
    priced by the pool's own oracle.  Borrowed-out funds still count:
    they are still owed to suppliers.

    "Fund USDC" = the USDC balance of the FUND classic account only.
    The fund's backstop shares do NOT count: they sit inside the
    contracts being covered and take bad debt first.

Backstop is not covered (DECISION 2026-11-17; replaces the "cover 50%
of backstop" future option).  Backstop deposits carry no cover.
COVER_BACKSTOP in cover-hours.sh stays 0.

The fund is the backstop (DECISION 2026-11-17).  FUND is the only
allowlisted backstop depositor, so it receives the pool's whole backstop
take rate (50%).  Earnings stay inside the backstop position until swept
(see "Take-rate sweep"); each sweep raises the fund balance and so the
deposit limit.  The fund's own backstop deposit is fund money at risk,
not part of the reserve.  Removing FUND from the allowlist would let
anyone force_queue_withdrawal its shares.

Business hours (Eastern time, America/New_York, so DST is automatic):
    open   Admin Active  (set_status 0)
    closed Admin Frozen  (set_status 4)

    Frozen, not On-Ice: On-Ice (2) only blocks new borrowing, deposits
    would still go in.  Frozen blocks supply and borrowing; withdrawals,
    repayments, liquidations and auction fills still work
    (blnt-contracts-v3/docs/V2_SYSTEM_SPEC.md, V2-POOL-STATUS-001).
    Status 4 is also immune to the permissionless update_status.

    Hours (DECISION 2026-11-12): NYSE hours, Mon-Fri 09:30-16:00 ET.
    "If the banks aren't open, the pool isn't open": closed on any NYSE
    holiday OR Federal Reserve bank holiday (e.g. Good Friday is NYSE
    only; Columbus and Veterans Day are Fed only), and closes at 13:00
    on NYSE early-close days.  Dates in cover-holidays.txt, filled
    through 2027; extend it every year.

Opening check (every business day, before open):
    The pool only opens if the cover rule holds at that moment.  If the
    fund is short (prices rose, interest accrued, fund paid out), the
    pool stays frozen until the fund is topped up or deposits fall.
    It is not re-checked during the day: the supply caps bound intraday
    growth (below).

Supply caps (weekly):
    Caps can't move daily: after setup, every reserve change waits 7
    days (V2-POOL-CONFIG-001).  So caps are revised weekly, sized so the
    total at current prices plus headroom for XLM moves stays under the
    deposit limit expected a week out.  The daily check catches anything
    the caps miss.

Interest only (DECISION 2026-11-12): no BLNT emissions, plain USDC
backstop tier only.  A pool with no BLNT LP tier has zero weight and can
never enter the reward zone (V3_SYSTEM_SPEC.md 6.2), so neither the
pool nor its backstop earns BLNT.  Immutable: emissions later = new pool.

Access controller (DECISION 2026-11-12, reverses "option C rejected"):
Multisig address: `GDA5JJFUAGFBNT4BMEGASY7EWBJFOWL2VBXMYY6QKCEOZMXHX6BBXYOB` . 
backstop deposits must be allowlisted.  The only mechanism is the v3
access controller, bound immutably at deploy, and the same controller
gates supply/borrow (bits 0/1) and backstop deposit (bit 2)
(backstop/src/access.rs, pool/src/access.rs).  No production controller
exists in blnt-contracts-v3 (test mocks only), so we write one:
    minimal, NOT upgradeable
    __constructor(admin)
    set_backstop_depositor(user, allowed)     admin only
    permissions(pool, user) -> 3, or 7 if user is allowlisted
        (supply + borrow open to everyone; ignores `pool`)
    admin = the fund's 2-of-3 multisig, NOT the hot pool-admin key
Controller failure blocks supply/borrow/backstop deposit only;
withdraw, repay and Q4W never call it.  Removing a depositor lets anyone
force_queue_withdrawal their shares.  Controller address and trust
model must be disclosed on the site.
Not used for the deposit limit (option C): caps + daily check do that.

4. Backstop deposit
-------------------

First allowlist the fund (step 3a):

    ./add-depositor-covered.sh $FUND     -> paste into refractor.space

Then the fund deposits, as the transaction source (2 signatures, via
Refractor):

    inv $BACKSTOP -- deposit --tier FirstLoss --from $FUND --pool $POOL \
        --amount 100000000          # 10 USDC

This 10 USDC leaves the fund account, so after it the fund must still
hold >= 90 USDC of reserve.

Weekly cap revision
-------------------

The pool admin is the pool-status-manager, owned by FUND, so a cap change
is a fund transaction signed in Refractor (2 of 3); applying it 7 days
later is permissionless.  Script: revise-caps-covered.sh (2026-11-20).

    ./revise-caps-covered.sh             report: fund, limit (fund / 0.9),
                                         deposits, caps, prices, queued
                                         changes, suggested caps
    ./revise-caps-covered.sh build XLM=160 USTRY=27 USDC=43
                                         one fund XDR per asset in xdr/,
                                         consecutive sequence numbers:
                                         submit IN ORDER, nothing else from
                                         the fund in between
    ./revise-caps-covered.sh apply       after 7 days: set_reserve each
                                         unlocked change (from xbull)

Rule: caps' total value with XLM +30% stays under the limit; build refuses
otherwise (--force overrides).  Only supply_cap changes; the rest of the
config is sent as it is (changing the interest curve resets ir_mod).  One
queued change per asset: a second fails until the first is applied or
cancelled (manager cancel_set_reserve, a fund XDR; not scripted).  Caps
only need changing when the fund's balance changes (sweep or top-up).  If
the fund will SHRINK, lower the caps first and keep the pool frozen until
the new caps apply and deposits are under the limit.


Take-rate sweep
---------------

The backstop's take-rate earnings grow the fund's backstop position, not
the fund account.  To move them into the reserve, periodically (e.g.
monthly, before a weekly cap revision):

    * read the fund's backstop position value vs. its principal
    * queue_withdrawal of the earned part only (FUND source, Refractor)
    * after the queue period, withdraw to FUND (Refractor again)
    * the new FUND balance counts from the next opening check and cap
      revision

Keep the principal in place so the backstop stays funded.

Daily operation: cover-hours.sh
-------------------------------

    cover-hours.sh check     print deposits, fund, required; exit 0 if covered
    cover-hours.sh tick      cron entry (every 5 min): freeze outside
                             hours; at/after open, run check and open only
                             if covered
    cover-hours.sh hold      incident: freeze now and stop tick reopening
    cover-hours.sh release   remove the hold (tick reopens next business
                             interval if covered)

tick prints only when it acts or something is wrong, so cron's mail is
the alert.  A failed opening check is reported once per day.
